When websites expand to hundreds of URLs or multiple regional sub-domains, organic visibility quickly decays without rigorous content governance.

## Key Pillars of Enterprise SEO Hygiene

1. **Structured Data and Schema Markup**: Ensure article, organization, and product schemas are automated within your CMS templates.
2. **Accessibility (WCAG Basics)**: Alt text, proper heading hierarchies (H1 to H4), and high-contrast color choices directly enhance crawlability and user engagement.
3. **Continuous Auditing**: Schedule automated crawls using Screaming Frog and Ahrefs to detect broken links, duplicate canonicals, and metadata drift before they impact rankings.

With proper governance in place, publishing speed increases without sacrificing search engine trust.
